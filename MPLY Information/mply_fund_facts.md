## Stated investment strategy (exact wording, official site)
"Monopolistic Attributes" the fund screens for: "Brand dominance, Regulatory
exclusivity, Industry concentration, Historical antitrust reviews,
Monopolistic or Oligopolistic economic rents, High barriers to market entry,
Pricing power, Vertically integrated and control of the supply chain,
Benefits from network effects, Protected intellectual property or patents."

Companies may qualify individually (monopoly) or collectively with a small
set of competitors (oligopoly). Actively managed; invests in US and non-US
stocks across market caps with these characteristics, per stockanalysis.com's
summary of the fund's stated approach.
